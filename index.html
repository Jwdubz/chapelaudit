thelittlevegaschapel.com · reviewed on a phone · October 2026

I went through your site the way a couple planning their wedding would, on a phone. Here's what I saw.

The short version

Your homepage does the important things right. “Book Your Wedding” and “Reserve Your Date” go straight to online booking, your phone number is tappable, and your packages are one tap away. What's holding it back is how long it takes to show up on a phone.

What I found

01Costing you most

On a phone, couples wait a long time before they see anything.

What I saw

In my phone test, the first thing appeared after 23.2 seconds, and the main picture took 41.0 seconds.

Why it matters

Couples planning a Vegas wedding are often comparing several chapels at once. Most won't wait that long. They tap back and book the chapel that loaded first.

The fix

Resize and compress the homepage images and hold back code that isn't needed for the first screen, so the top of the page shows first.

02Worth fixing

The page jumps around while it loads.

What I saw

Parts of the homepage shift as it loads.

Why it matters

Someone reaching for “Book Your Wedding” can tap the wrong thing. That's a small annoyance right at the moment they're ready to commit.

The fix

Reserve space for images and badges before they load so nothing moves under a thumb.

03Worth fixing

Some of your buttons are hard to read.

What I saw

In my phone test, 13 pieces of button and heading text didn't stand out enough from their background.

Why it matters

Those are the words that tell people what to tap next.

The fix

Darken or enlarge that text so it stands clearly apart from what's behind it.

04Quick cleanup

Smaller cleanup.

What I saw

A few review-site badges (like your TripAdvisor link) have no label for people using screen readers, and the homepage loads a lot of extra code that doesn't help visitors.

The fix

Add short labels to the badges and trim code the homepage doesn't use.

What's working

Clear booking buttons that go straight to a real calendar, a tappable phone number, your address and hours on the page, and packages for every kind of ceremony.

What I couldn't measure

How many people visit, where you rank, and how many people start booking and don't finish. That's unmeasured, so nothing here is guessed.
