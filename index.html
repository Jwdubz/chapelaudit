What your site is costing you — The Little Vegas Chapel

I'm Jarrett, a web designer here in Las Vegas. I went through your site the way a couple planning their wedding would, on a phone. Here's what I saw.

The short version: your homepage does the important things right. "Book Your Wedding" and "Reserve Your Date" go straight to online booking, your phone number is tappable, and your packages are one tap away. What's holding it back is how long it takes to show up on a phone.

1. On a phone, couples wait a long time before they see anything. In my phone test, the first thing appeared after 23.2 seconds, and the main picture took 41.0 seconds. Couples planning a Vegas wedding are often comparing several chapels at once. Most won't wait that long. They tap back and book the chapel that loaded first.

2. The page jumps around while it loads. Parts of the homepage shift as it loads, so someone reaching for "Book Your Wedding" can tap the wrong thing. That's a small annoyance right at the moment they're ready to commit.

3. Some of your buttons are hard to read. In my phone test, 13 pieces of button and heading text didn't stand out enough from their background. Those are the words that tell people what to tap next.

4. Smaller cleanup: a few review-site badges (like your TripAdvisor link) have no label for people using screen readers, and the homepage loads a lot of extra code that doesn't help visitors.

What's working: clear booking buttons that go straight to a real calendar, a tappable phone number, your address and hours on the page, and packages for every kind of ceremony.

What we couldn't measure: how many people visit, where you rank, and how many people start booking and don't finish. That's unmeasured, so nothing here is guessed.
